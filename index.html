How a day works

A guided flow asks one thing at a time, in the order the day happens, and offers to apply each answer to the rest of your crew.

  1. 01

    Start the DTR

    Confirm today's date, or pick another, then tick who's working.

  2. 02

    Call times

    Enter a call time and apply it to the rest of the crew, with checkboxes for anyone different.

  3. 03

    Lunch

    Lunch end fills in 30 minutes after the start. Late meals are flagged as meal penalties.

  4. 04

    Wrap

    Set wrap for everyone, or skip someone still working and come back later.

  5. 05

    Send to payroll

    Download the filled DTR, email it to payroll, and mark the day as sent.

Features

Everything a department needs from first day to final timesheet, in one app.

Onboard

Set up the HOD's details, department, production, payroll email, job categories and the hours rules: when a meal is due, overtime and double time, and the start of the payroll week.

Crew invites

Share one invite link by QR code, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook or X. Crew add their own name, phone and email.

Personal invite codes

Each crew member can get their own 6-character code by email, text or WhatsApp. It links their login to their entry, and you approve or reject every link.

Guided DTR

Stage-by-stage prompts in crew order, a grid for corrections, and the crew always sorted by your job categories.

Call sheets

Upload each day's call sheet as a PDF or image. Crew view it in the app and download it.

My DTR

Crew see their own times for each day, read-only, and nobody else's.

Wrap time requests

Crew can submit their own wrap time. You approve it straight into the DTR or reject it, and they see the answer.

Your production's forms

Upload the production's own fillable DTR and timesheet PDFs. The app matches each box to the right value, and you can check it with a test fill.

Weekly timesheets

One timecard per crew member, built from the week's DTRs, with straight time, overtime, double time and meal penalties. Download it as a PDF or CSV.

Send to payroll

One button saves the PDF and opens an email to payroll with the subject and summary written. On phones it can attach the PDF for you.

Permissions

The HOD decides what Co-HODs, admins and crew can each do, one switch at a time.

Numbered downloads

Downloading again never overwrites an earlier file: you get name (1), name (2) and so on.

Who does what

HOD

Runs the department. Can do everything, and sets everyone else's permissions.

Co-HOD

Shares the load: DTRs, crew, invites, job categories and Onboard.

Admin

Keeps the DTR, approves wrap times, uploads call sheets and sends to payroll.

Crew

Sees the call sheet and their own times, keeps their details up to date, and submits wrap times.

How hours are counted

Hours worked are wrap minus call minus lunch. Overtime and double time start at the daily thresholds you set in Onboard.

A meal penalty is flagged when lunch starts later than the meal-due time after call. Times past midnight follow the film convention, so a 2 am wrap is 26:00, and the pickers go up to 36:54.
